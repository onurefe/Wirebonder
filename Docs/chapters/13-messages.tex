\chapter{Messages and Troubleshooting}
\label{ch:messages}

Everything the machine tells you arrives as a message on the display. This
chapter lists every message it can produce, what caused it, and what to do.

\section{The three kinds of message}

How a message behaves tells you how serious it is, before you have even read
it.

\begin{center}\small
\begin{tabular}{@{}L{26mm}L{40mm}L{50mm}@{}}
\toprule
\textbf{Kind} & \textbf{How it clears} & \textbf{What it means} \\
\midrule
Notification & By itself, after about five seconds, or on any key press &
  Something worked. No action needed. \\
\addlinespace
Warning & On any key press. Headed \msg{WARNING} &
  Something did not work, but the machine is fine. Read it, clear it, try
  again. \\
\addlinespace
Error & It does not clear. Headed \msg{ERROR} &
  Something serious failed. The keypad is locked and the machine will not
  move. Press \keycap{RESET}. \\
\bottomrule
\end{tabular}
\end{center}

\begin{wbnote}
The key press that clears a warning is consumed by the warning itself --- it
does not also do whatever that key normally does. Press it once to clear, then
again to act.
\end{wbnote}

\begin{wbwarning}
An error latches. It cannot be dismissed, it cannot be replaced by a later
message, and no key will restore the menus. This is deliberate: a machine that
has failed a critical operation does not know where its axes are, so it refuses
to move. The only way out is \keycap{RESET} or a power cycle, either of which
re-homes the machine.
\end{wbwarning}

\section{Problems that produce no message}

Some faults are silent because nothing has failed from the machine's point of
view --- it did exactly what it was told.

\begin{center}\small
\begin{tabular}{@{}L{45mm}L{78mm}@{}}
\toprule
\textbf{Symptom} & \textbf{Check} \\
\midrule

The machine does nothing when I press the mouse button &
The machine is not idle, or an error is latched. Look at the display: if an
error is showing, press \keycap{RESET}. Also confirm the display is on the
Parameter Edit page with a configuration loaded. \\
\addlinespace

The display is blank at power-up &
The machine has no power, or the display cable is loose. This is a service
fault, not an operating one. \\
\addlinespace

The tool descends but never touches down &
\menuitem{Search 1} may be below the workpiece, so the search never starts from
above it. Raise \menuitem{Search 1} and confirm \menuitem{Overtravel} is
negative. \\
\addlinespace

Bond quality changed and nothing was edited &
Run \keycap{TEST}. A shifted resonance or a fallen Q points at the transducer
or the tool mounting rather than the recipe. Then run \keycap{SETUP} to confirm
force is still calibrated. \\
\addlinespace

My parameter edits were lost at power-up &
Edits are not saved until you press \keycap{SAVE} on the Parameter Edit page.
The same applies to the Settings page. \\
\addlinespace

A hotkey shows \msg{Not used by protocol} &
That parameter does not exist in the active bonding mode. See
Appendix~\ref{app:defaults} for which modes show what. \\

\bottomrule
\end{tabular}
\end{center}
