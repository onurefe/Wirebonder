\chapter[Using the K\&S Service Manual]{Using the \ksmanual}
\label{app:ks}

This machine is a \basemachine\ with its control electronics replaced. The
mechanics are the original ones, so the \ksmanual\ remains a useful and
accurate reference --- for the mechanics. It is not accurate about anything the
controller does.

This appendix says which is which.

\section{What the \ksmanual\ is still right about}

Everything mechanical, because none of it changed:

\begin{itemize}[leftmargin=*]
\item The bond head, the bond lever, and their adjustment.
\item Fitting, aligning and replacing the bonding tool.
\item Wire threading, the wire guides, the clamp mechanism, spool mounting and
  tension.
\item The workholder and stage mechanics.
\item Chassis, installation, mounting and environmental requirements.
\item Mechanical spares, consumables and part numbers.
\item Bonding schedules, and the physics of what force, power and time do to a
  bond.
\item Bond inspection and pull-test practice.
\end{itemize}

\section{What it is wrong about}

Everything downstream of the control card. Use this manual instead.

\begin{center}\small
\begin{tabular}{@{}L{40mm}L{82mm}@{}}
\toprule
\textbf{Topic} & \textbf{Why it differs} \\
\midrule

Control panel and display &
Different hardware entirely. This machine has a 20$\times$4 character display
and a menu system; key functions, menu structure and screen layouts do not
correspond. See Chapters~\ref{ch:controls} and~\ref{ch:navigation}. \\
\addlinespace

Error and status messages &
Entirely different message set. Chapter~\ref{ch:messages} is the only valid
list. \\
\addlinespace

Parameter storage &
This machine stores up to 32 named configurations per bonding mode in
non-volatile memory, with a permanent \menuitem{DEFAULT}. See
Chapter~\ref{ch:configurations}. \\
\addlinespace

Calibration procedures &
The force, ultrasonic and tachometer calibrations in
Chapter~\ref{ch:calibration} are specific to this controller and have no
4523AD equivalent. \\
\addlinespace

Ultrasonic generator &
This machine sweeps for resonance before every weld, tracks it, and delivers a
measured \emph{energy} rather than running for a set time. The scan settings,
the reported Q, and the tail assist have no 4523AD counterpart. \\
\addlinespace

Homing and power-up &
This machine homes its Y axis automatically at power-up and refuses to move if
that fails. See Chapter~\ref{ch:powerup}. \\
\addlinespace

Electrical schematics, wiring, fuses, supply &
The control electronics are new. The 4523AD electrical documentation does not
describe this machine. \\
\addlinespace

Regulatory markings &
The base machine's original conformity does not carry over to the machine as
rebuilt. \\

\bottomrule
\end{tabular}
\end{center}

\begin{wbwarning}
Do not use the \ksmanual's electrical documentation to diagnose or repair this
machine. The control card is not the one it describes, and its wiring,
connectors and voltages are different.
\end{wbwarning}

\section{Parameters: what maps and what does not}

The first three parameter screens follow the 4523AD schedule and the names
match, so a 4523AD bonding schedule can be entered here directly ---
with the cautions in Chapter~\ref{ch:tuning}.

\begin{center}\small
\begin{tabular}{@{}L{34mm}L{88mm}@{}}
\toprule
\textbf{Screens} & \textbf{Relationship to the 4523AD} \\
\midrule
1/6 First bond    & Follows the 4523AD schedule; names match \\
2/6 Loop          & Follows the 4523AD schedule; names match \\
3/6 Second bond   & Follows the 4523AD schedule; names match \\
4/6 Heights       & This controller. Similar concepts, different limits \\
5/6 Timing        & This controller only --- no 4523AD equivalent \\
6/6 Force \& U/S  & This controller only --- no 4523AD equivalent \\
\bottomrule
\end{tabular}
\end{center}

\begin{wbnote}
Force is in grams on both machines and transfers directly --- but only once
\keycap{SETUP} has been run here, since until then this machine's commanded
force is uncorrected. Power and energy do \emph{not} transfer, because the
ultrasonic drive and transducer are not the same.
\end{wbnote}
