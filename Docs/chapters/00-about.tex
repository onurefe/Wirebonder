\chapter{About This Manual}

This manual describes how to operate the \productname\ \productdescriptor: its
controls, its menus, its bonding modes, and the parameters that determine bond
quality.

\section*{Who it is for}

The operator at the machine. It assumes you understand what a wire bond is and
why one might be good or bad, but not that you have used this particular
machine before.

\section*{This manual and the \ksmanual}

The \productname\ is built on \basemachine\ mechanics with a new control card
and new firmware. That split determines which document you should reach for.

The rule of thumb: \textbf{if it happens on the screen or under software
control, it is here; if it is something you touch, adjust or replace with your
hands, it is in the \ksmanual.}

\begin{wbnote}
The bonding parameters follow the 4523AD parameter schedule, so bonding
schedules developed for a 4523AD transfer to this machine directly. The names
match. Where this machine adds parameters the 4523AD does not have --- the
ultrasonic scan settings, the tail assist, and the timing group --- those are
described here and only here.
\end{wbnote}

\begin{wbwarning}
Where the two documents disagree about anything the controller does --- button
behaviour, parameter ranges, the bonding sequence, error messages --- this
manual is correct and the \ksmanual\ is not. The control electronics are not
the ones its author described. Appendix~\ref{app:ks} lists the known
differences.
\end{wbwarning}

\section*{How it is organised}

\begin{description}[leftmargin=!,labelwidth=42mm,style=nextline,font=\normalfont\bfseries]
\item[Part I --- Getting to Know the Machine]
  The axes, the controls, the display, and what happens at power-up. Read this
  first.
\item[Part II --- Operating]
  Navigating the menus, managing configurations, the four bonding modes, and
  running a bond.
\item[Part III --- Setup and Tuning]
  The three calibrations you perform from the panel, and how to turn a bad bond
  into a good one.
\item[Part IV --- Reference]
  Every parameter, every message, the specifications, and a one-page quick
  reference to print and keep at the machine.
\end{description}
