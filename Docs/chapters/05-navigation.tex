\chapter{Finding Your Way Around}
\label{ch:navigation}

The machine has seven screens. You will spend almost all your time on two of
them: the configuration list and the parameter editor.

\section{The screen map}

\begin{center}\small
\begin{tabular}{@{}L{40mm}L{16mm}L{62mm}@{}}
\toprule
\textbf{From} & \textbf{Press} & \textbf{You get} \\
\midrule
\multirow{4}{=}{\raggedright Configuration Select \newline
                 \emph{(where the machine starts)}}
  & \keycap{ENTER} & Parameter Edit, with that configuration loaded \\
  & \keycap{ADD}     & Add Configuration \\
  & \keycap{ESC/DEL} & Delete Confirm \\
  & \keycap{SAVE}    & Settings \\
\addlinespace
\multirow{2}{=}{\raggedright Parameter Edit}
  & \keycap{SAVE}    & saves --- or Save As, if the configuration is
                       \menuitem{DEFAULT} \\
  & \keycap{ESC/DEL} & back to Configuration Select \\
\addlinespace
Add Configuration  & \keycap{ENTER} & Parameter Edit, with the new configuration \\
Save As            & \keycap{ENTER} & Parameter Edit, saved under the new name \\
Delete Confirm     & \keycap{ENTER} & back to Configuration Select \\
Settings           & \keycap{ESC/DEL} & back to Configuration Select \\
Force Setup        & \keycap{ENTER} & back where you came from \\
\bottomrule
\end{tabular}
\end{center}

\keycap{ESC/DEL} always goes back one step, except on the configuration list
where there is nowhere further back to go --- there it deletes instead.

\begin{wbwarning}
On the configuration list, \keycap{ESC/DEL} is the delete key. It asks for
confirmation first, but be aware that the key which means ``go back'' elsewhere
means ``delete this'' here.
\end{wbwarning}

\section{Configuration Select}

The screen the machine starts on.

\begin{lcdscreen}
\begin{lcdverb}
Mode: Semi Auto  1/4
*DEFAULT
 FINE ALU
 THICK GOLD
\end{lcdverb}
\end{lcdscreen}

$\leftarrow$ and $\rightarrow$ change which bonding mode's configurations are
listed --- each mode keeps its own set. $\uparrow$ and $\downarrow$ move the
\texttt{*}. \keycap{ENTER} or \keycap{LOAD} loads the marked configuration.

\section{Parameter Edit}

Where you tune a recipe. The 31 parameters are spread over six screens;
$\leftarrow$ and $\rightarrow$ move between them, and the screen number is
shown top right.

\begin{wbnote}
$\leftarrow$ and $\rightarrow$ do not repeat when held on this screen. Press
once per screen.
\end{wbnote}

$\uparrow$ and $\downarrow$ move the \texttt{*}; \keycap{$+$} and
\keycap{$-$} change the marked value. The four hotkey pairs jump straight to
common parameters without navigating to them --- see Chapter~\ref{ch:controls}.

\section{Typing a name}

Both Add Configuration and Save As use the same editor. Names are ten
characters, chosen from \texttt{A--Z}, \texttt{0--9}, space, \texttt{-} and
\texttt{\_}.

\begin{center}\small
\begin{tabular}{@{}L{34mm}L{88mm}@{}}
\toprule
\textbf{Key} & \textbf{Action} \\
\midrule
$\leftarrow$ $\rightarrow$ & Move to another character position \\
$\uparrow$ $\downarrow$, \keycap{$+$} \keycap{$-$} & Change the character \\
\keycap{ENTER}   & Accept the name \\
\keycap{ESC/DEL} & Cancel \\
\bottomrule
\end{tabular}
\end{center}

New names start as \menuitem{NEWCONFIG}. Unlike everywhere else, $\leftarrow$
and $\rightarrow$ \emph{do} repeat here, so you can hold them to run along the
name.

\section{Confirming a deletion}

\begin{lcdscreen}
\begin{lcdverb}
Deleting: FINE ALU
Are you sure?
       Y      N
\end{lcdverb}
\end{lcdscreen}

$\uparrow$ or $\leftarrow$ selects \menuitem{Y}; $\downarrow$ or $\rightarrow$
selects \menuitem{N}. \keycap{ENTER} carries out your answer.
